San Antonio Funeral Carriage

San Antonio is one of the most deeply military cities in America — home to Joint Base San Antonio, Fort Sam Houston National Cemetery, Lackland Air Force Base, and Randolph Air Force Base. It is a city that understands honor, service, and the weight of a proper farewell. San Antonio Funeral Carriage, part of the trusted White Horse and Carriage Company, is based right here — and we are proud to serve the families, veterans, and communities of this city with the dignity they deserve. We provide horse-drawn hearses, black caissons for military and veteran services, family carriages, and the riderless horse — all handled with the care and professionalism that San Antonio's families have come to expect from us.

Frequently Asked Questions

San Antonio Families Ask Us

Do you serve Fort Sam Houston National Cemetery?

+
Yes. We have provided horse-drawn hearse and caisson services at Fort Sam Houston National Cemetery and are experienced with military funeral protocols. We coordinate directly with funeral homes to integrate into the service seamlessly — families don't have to manage the logistics themselves.

What areas of San Antonio do you serve?

+
We serve all of San Antonio and Bexar County, including surrounding communities such as New Braunfels, Boerne, Seguin, San Marcos, Schertz, Converse, Universal City, and Helotes. We are based in San Antonio, so we know this city and its cemeteries well. Contact us with your location and we will confirm availability.

Do you provide military funeral carriages in San Antonio?

+
Absolutely. San Antonio is Military City USA and we are proud to serve this community. We provide the black caisson, riderless horse, and white hearse for veteran and military services — coordinating with funeral homes and working within military funeral protocol. The riderless horse and black caisson are also available for any family, not just military.

What is a riderless horse and who can have one?

+
The riderless horse — boots reversed in the stirrups — is a centuries-old symbol of a warrior who will ride no more. While most associated with military funerals, any family can request a riderless horse for any loved one. It is not limited to veterans or military families. Family animals are also welcome to participate in the procession — the family is responsible for handling their own animals.

How far in advance should I book?

+
As early as possible. Families who are pre-planning can note their desire for a horse-drawn procession in their funeral planning packet so the funeral home can contact us when the time comes. Funeral homes sometimes reach out 48 hours before a service and we do our best to accommodate everyone. Custom plumes require at least two weeks notice; a team of two horses requires at least one week.

Do you work with San Antonio funeral homes?

+
Yes, and we prefer it. Working through the funeral director takes the coordination burden entirely off the family on one of the hardest days of their lives. We are experienced working with funeral homes across San Antonio and Bexar County. Funeral directors are welcome to contact us directly to discuss arrangements.

Can we customize the carriage?

+
Yes. Custom colored plumes, flag draping, and carriage decorations are all available. We encourage families to provide their own decorations — passed through the funeral director to us at setup — so the result reflects exactly their vision. We have incorporated American flags, Puerto Rican flags, team colors, and custom floral arrangements. Whatever matters to your family, we will make it work.

What if the weather is bad on the day of service?

+
If we can get there safely, we will be there. We have operated in cold, rain, and difficult Texas weather because we understand that a funeral cannot be rescheduled lightly. In over ten years of service, we have had to cancel or reschedule extremely rarely — and never from our end. San Antonio families can count on us to show up.
